\blueprintfrontmatter

\begin{abstract}
This blueprint maps \code{flt-cyclotomic-nt}, the number-theoretic core shared by
the engine and the regular-prime route. It develops four exports:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a kernel-checkable Bernoulli-numerator (irregularity) certificate;
  \item Herbrand's theorem, in eigenspace form, from the class-group Stickelberger
    relation (assembled from the single-prime core of \code{flt-stickelberger});
  \item the cyclotomic-unit index $[E:C] = \hplus$ (Washington Thm~8.2); and
  \item the Cauchy--Genocchi Case~I criterion.
\end{itemize}
The single-prime Stickelberger relation is consumed from \code{flt-stickelberger}
and appears here as an external leaf. Nodes link by \code{\textbackslash lean} to
their declarations (\code{CyclotomicNT}, \code{FaithSpike}, \code{IrrCertNat}, and
a few top-level names).
\end{abstract}
